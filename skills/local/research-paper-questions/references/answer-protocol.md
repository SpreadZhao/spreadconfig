# Answer Protocol

## Evidence Layers

Structure every substantial answer with the layers that actually apply:

### Paper evidence

Quote or paraphrase the exact claim and cite source block IDs, page numbers, equations, figures, tables, experiments, or appendices. Distinguish what the authors state from what their results demonstrate.

### External evidence

Link directly to sources and record title, author or publisher, date when relevant, URL, access date, and why the source is credible. Prefer primary literature and official material. For current claims such as citation status, software behavior, or venue information, verify on the web.

### Inference

Explain the reasoning bridge and assumptions. Use calibrated language such as “this suggests” or “a likely reason is.”

### Uncertainty

Name missing evidence, conflicting sources, extraction uncertainty, or interpretations that cannot be distinguished.

Do not force empty sections into a short answer; keep the evidence category explicit in the note and ledger.

## Answer Note

Use a paper-scoped filename such as `<paper-id>-Q0001 · <short-question>.md`. Include:

- question and relationship links;
- concise answer first;
- evidence layers;
- effect on the Reader's current model;
- remaining uncertainty and proposed follow-up;
- source list with direct links;
- answer status and `later_section` flag.

Use source-language quotations sparingly. Prefer precise paraphrase and respect source quotation limits.

## Research Log

Append useful searches and inspected sources to a thread note rather than depending on browser history. Record unsuccessful searches when they explain why a question remains open. Avoid copying entire external articles or papers into the workspace; save citations, relevant evidence, and authorized local sources.

## Quality Gate

An answer is ready for Reader review when it addresses the exact question, identifies its evidence type, exposes uncertainty, and provides enough provenance for verification. A long literature survey that never answers the question does not pass.
